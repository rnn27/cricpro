# 03 — v0.1 results and findings

v0.1 is a Python reference implementation of the speed pipeline (`speed-core/`), validated on
synthetic deliveries with known speed. It is **not** yet an Android app: the algorithm is the
risky part and could be tested here; the Android shell could not be compiled in this environment.

## What was built
- Timestamps from a sidecar JSON written by the recorder (sensor timestamps), never the fps label.
- Calibration: least-squares similarity from 4 stump taps (assumes camera square-on to pitch).
- Ball candidates: median background + blobs inside the calibrated region.
- Trajectory: RANSAC seed (gravity-plausible, 12–50 m/s forward) then cubic fit per axis.
- Output: release speed, average speed, 95% uncertainty, HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW confidence with reasons.
- Uncertainty: Monte Carlo over tap noise (1 px), bootstrap fit noise, camera-alignment prior
  (1.5% end-to-end scale difference), +1% if timing is only assumed.

## Synthetic results (1 px tap noise, 240 fps unless noted)
| condition | mean abs error | reported ±95% | true value inside |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| square-on | 0.34 km/h | 1.7 | 100% |
| yaw ~1% end to end | 0.69 km/h | 1.7 | 100% |
| yaw ~4% end to end | 3.75 km/h | 1.8 | 0% |
| square-on, 120 fps | 0.40 km/h | 1.8 | 100% |
| square-on, fps label 0.4% off | 0.17 km/h | 3.3 | 100% |

## Findings
1. Tracking is accurate: with perfect calibration the error was under 0.1 km/h.
2. Calibration is the bottleneck. A 4-point homography from stump taps is ill-conditioned
   (all points sit in a 20 m x 0.7 m strip): 1 px of tap noise gave an 11 km/h error.
   Fixed by switching to a similarity fit (8 equations, 4 unknowns).
3. A camera that is not square-on biases speed by about the end-to-end scale change and cannot
   be detected from stump taps at ~1 px noise. The reported uncertainty is then overconfident.
   **±1 km/h needs verified alignment — printed fiducial markers at both ends are the top
   item for v0.2.**
4. The gravity cross-check must include air drag (nominal k = 0.0063 /m); without it a correct
   track looked 16% off.
5. A first synthetic camera had non-square pixels, which produced a fake -1 km/h bias;
   the harness was corrected. (Reminder: validate the validator.)

## Not yet tested
Real footage, lens distortion, rolling shutter, ball off the centre-line plane, clutter/sky
backgrounds, ball hidden by the bowler's hand at release, compression beyond one mp4 test.
