# Journey log

How cricpro got where it is. One entry per working session in `docs/journey/`,
numbered in order. Each entry records: what we decided, why, what we found, and what is open.

Convention: every meaningful change to the project gets (a) a commit, and (b) when a decision
or finding is involved, a journey entry in the same or the next commit. Tag releases (`v0.1.0`).

| 02 | [v0 scope and decisions](docs/journey/02-v0-scope-and-decisions.md) | What v0 includes, fps handling, calibration ideas, accuracy target |
